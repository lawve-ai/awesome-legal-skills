# Find Case Law — stated coverage per court

Read from <https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/courts-and-tribunals> on
**27 August 2026**. The range is the register's earliest-to-latest holding for
that court. The register's own caveat, quoted: *"Coverage within that range may
not be complete — not every judgment is sent to us for publication."*

Holdings grow. When a verdict turns on a boundary, re-read the live page
rather than this snapshot.

## Not held at all

| Citation form | Position |
|---|---|
| `[YYYY] UKHL n` | House of Lords judgments are not on Find Case Law. UKSC holdings begin 2009. A UKHL citation can never be verified — or impugned — from this register. |
| Law-report citations (`[1978] 1 WLR 779`, `(1980) 71 Cr App R 1` …) | Not resolvable by citation. Find the neutral citation by party name where one exists (post-2001); otherwise verify in the printed series or a subscription service. |

## What is not covered at all

Scotland and Northern Ireland (their courts publish elsewhere), magistrates'
courts, and most first-instance tribunal decisions outside the chambers listed
above. A citation from any of these is OUTSIDE COVERAGE by definition.

## Official archives to check by hand where the register cannot answer

Pointers only — these are search services, not citation-addressable registers,
so the tool deliberately does not automate them (see SKILL.md, "Why the
register only"):

| Gap | Official archive |
|---|---|
| House of Lords judgments 1996–2009 | <https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/ld/ldjudgmt.htm> (refuses scripted access — use a browser) |
| EAT decisions before 2021 (`UKEAT/nnnn/yy`) | <https://www.gov.uk/employment-appeal-tribunal-decisions> |
| First-instance Employment Tribunal decisions (Feb 2017 –) | <https://www.gov.uk/employment-tribunal-decisions> |
| UT(IAC) and other tribunal decisions | <https://tribunalsdecisions.service.gov.uk> |

BAILII is deliberately absent from this table: it is a mirror rather than an
official record, and its robots.txt disallows automated access to the
directories holding England and Wales judgments.
